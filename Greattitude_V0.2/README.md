# Greattitude V0.2

A small iOS and Mac app for noticing what’s good, then revisiting it later.

Open `Greattitude.xcodeproj` in Xcode, pick an iPhone simulator **or** My Mac, and press Run.

## What changed from V0.1

- Home is two actions (new experience above revisit), a short retention note, an About info control, and quiet **Reminder settings** / **Privacy** links.
- **Revisit** is a dated list of question 1 answers, oldest first. You choose the entry.
- Inside an entry: **Reflect on this**, **Edit experience**, **Delete experience**.
- Reflection reveals answers in order: first two, then the third, then all four, then **Finish**.
- Daily reminders still exist. A revisit reminder opens the list, not a forced next item.
